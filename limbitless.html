Limbitless

Limbitless Solutions Inc. is a non-profit company that strives to empower individuals and increase accessibility through their expressive technology. As a Web Development Intern, I gained hands-on experience across the fill spectrum of web design and development. Working closely with a collaborative team, I grew both technically and professionally. My time at Limbitless was incredibly rewarding, and I truly loved being part of such a meaningful mission.

Projects

The Latest at Limbitless Page

This page was created to serve as a centralized hub for the company's blogs, news, and press articles. This makes it easier when users have to find specific content instead of having to sift through multiple pages. I was primarily responsible for designing and developing the page, with occasional support from my teammates.

ENGAGE page

The ENGAGE page showcases all outreach and STEAM program information. I was tasked with editing any outdated images, text, and redesigning the hero section for a more refreshed look.

Task Page

This project focused on redesigning the employee portal used for delegating tasks to each team. While a previous intern began the initial design, I was responsible for building upon and advancing the design work in Figma.

Works Page

Although I was only involved in the early drafting stages of this page, its purpose was to showcase all the innovative technologies the company had to offer. I created several Figma mockups to support and guide the newer interns during development.

Outreach

In addition to contributing my coding and design skills, I supported the company's outreach efforts by representing them at the Disney Accessibility Expo. It was an incredible event where I had the opportunity to explore a variety of innovative inventions and connect with many talented individuals.
