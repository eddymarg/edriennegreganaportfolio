The Organization

What is Limbitless?

Limbitless Solutions Inc. is a nonprofit dedicated to empowering individuals and increasing accessibility through expressive technology. From 3D-printed bionic arms to STEAM outreach programs, they work at the intersection of innovation and impact.

As a Web Development Intern, I gained hands-on experience across the full spectrum of web design and development — working closely with a collaborative team to build and improve pages on the company's live site.

My Contributions

The Latest at Limbitless

A centralized hub for the company's blogs, news, and press articles. I was primarily responsible for designing and developing this page, making it easier for users to find content without sifting through multiple sections of the site.

ENGAGE Page

The ENGAGE page showcases all outreach and STEAM program information. I was tasked with updating outdated images and text, and redesigning the hero section for a more refreshed and accessible look.

Task Page

An internal employee portal used for delegating tasks across teams. While a previous intern began the initial design, I was responsible for building on and advancing the Figma design work to prepare it for development.

Works Page & Outreach

I created Figma mockups to support newer interns in developing a page showcasing the company's innovative technologies. I also represented Limbitless at the Disney Accessibility Expo — an incredible experience connecting with inventors and advocates in the accessibility space.
