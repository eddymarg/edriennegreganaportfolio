Team Project · Mobile App

Wanderlust

A travel planning app that takes the stress out of vacation itineraries — find, organize, and go.

Role Designer & Developer
Platform iOS (Swift)
Tools Figma · Xcode · Swift
The Problem

Travel planning is scattered.

Planning a trip usually means juggling tabs — one for flights, one for hotels, another for things to do, a separate note for the itinerary. My mobile dev team set out to consolidate that experience into one focused app for activity discovery and trip organization.

Wanderlust lets users filter destinations by country, search for specific cities, and browse activity recommendations — all in one place, without the tab chaos.

What I Built

Features.

Country Filtering

Users can filter destinations by country, narrowing down the discovery experience to a specific region before diving into city-level planning.

City Search

A search function lets users jump directly to a specific city, pulling up relevant activities and highlights without browsing through every option.

Activity Recommendations

The app surfaces activity recommendations ranked by popularity and category, giving travelers a curated starting point instead of a blank page.

Roadmap: In-App Itinerary Planning

The next phase adds direct itinerary creation within the app — letting users save activities, map them to days, and export or share their plan with travel companions.
