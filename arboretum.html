The Organization

What is the Arboretum?

The UCF Arboretum is a student-led agricultural initiative that offers a space for students to engage with and learn about the natural environment. Through hands-on programming, events, and outreach, the organization promotes environmental education across campus.

As part of the Media & Marketing team, I was responsible for managing and implementing updates to the organization's website. Ensuring content, design, and functionality accurately reflected ongoing projects and events.

What I Did

My Contributions

Website Redesign & Digital Integration

One of the major projects I led was facilitating the digital integration of the Arboretum and UCF Facilities departments into a unified entity, Arboretum & Sustainability Initiatives. This required a complete merging of the existing WordPress site to reflect the expanded mission and incorporate new content from the Facilities team. Through ongoing meetings with stakeholders from both departments, I managed the merging of content and ensured a smooth transition across the site.

Marketing & Photography

In addition to my web work, I contributed to the Arboretum's marketing efforts by offering my photography skills and creative input for events and campaigns. Capturing moments from events helped give the organization a stronger visual presence and made it easier to connect with students across campus.
