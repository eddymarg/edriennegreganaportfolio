Arboretum

The Arboretum is a student-led agricultural initiative that offers a space for students to engage with and learn about the natural environment. During my time with the Arboretum, I was responsible for managing and implementing updates to the organization's website. As part of the Media & Marketing team, I ensured the site's content, design, and functionality accurately reflected ongoing projects and events, helping to promote environmental education and outreach across campus.

One of the major projects I led during my time at the Arboretum was facilitating the digital integration of the Arboretum and UCF Facilities departments into a unified entity known as Arboretum & Sustainability Initiatives. This transition required a complete redesign of the existing website to reflect the expanded mission and incorporate new content provided by the Facilties team. Through ongoing meetings, collaboration, and coordination with stakeholders from both sides, I helped manage the merging of information and ensured a smooth transition on the organization's WordPress site.

In addition to my work on the website, I also contributed to the Arboretum's marketing efforts by offering my photography skills and creative input for various events and campaigns.
