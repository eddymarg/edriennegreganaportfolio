ASIS: Lab Assistant

For our Senior Design project, my team was sponsored by Accenture to build upon an existing application aimed at optimizing research lab workflows. The goal of the project was to create a centralized platform that allowed lab personnel to monitor and control multiple laboratory machines from a single interface. We worked closely with Accenture mentors to align development with industry standards and real-world lab needs, ensuring the application would provide measurable improvements in efficiency and usability.
