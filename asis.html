The Project

What is ASIS?

For our Senior Design capstone, my team was sponsored by Accenture to improve an existing application aimed at optimizing research lab workflows. Lab personnel had to juggle multiple interfaces to monitor and control different machines. ASIS brought everything into one centralized platform.

We ran in two-week sprints with regular stakeholder reviews, working closely with Accenture mentors to ensure the product aligned with real industry needs and measurably improved lab efficiency.
