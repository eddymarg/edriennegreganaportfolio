Book Stock

An inventory tracker for book lovers because your collection deserves better than a simple note.

Role Designer & Developer
Platform Web (React)
Tools JavaScript · Firebase · VSCode
The Problem

Know your shelves.

Book lovers often struggle to keep track of what they own, what they've read, and what they want to read next. Book Stock is a personal inventory platform that gives readers a clean, searchable record of their entire collection.

The goal was to make the process as frictionless as possible starting with manual entry and image uploads, with a roadmap toward AI provided recommendations.

What I Built

Features.

Add & Manage Books

Users can add books to their collection and view them in a clean table layout. Built with Next.js, changes are reflected in real time via Firebase.

Image Uploads

Readers can attach a cover image to each entry, making their collection more visual and easier to browse at a glance.

Firebase Backend

Leveraged Firebase for real-time data storage and retrieval, keeping the architecture lightweight while enabling live updates across sessions.

Roadmap: AI Book Recommendations

The next step is AI provided recommendations assisting users with finding their next book obsession.
