Foodie, meets audiophile. SoundBites is the ultimate matchmaker for music lovers with an appetite. We don't just recommend restaurants, we compase an entire experience pairing your go-to genres with places that hit all the right notes. So turn up the volume, grab a fork, and get ready to eat to the beat.

Overview

Welcome to my newest passion project! As a fellow music nerd and foodie, I thought this would be the perfect project to appeal to both sides of my personality.

One day I was sitting at my desk thinking, "hmm I need to try some new food places instead of going to the same ones. How am I going to narrow down this search?" Magically, the idea of a play on words with music taste and taste profile gave life to SoundBites.

This may not be everyone's cup of tea, but my main audience for this project are people who love things like Spotify wrapped and personality tests. It gives them a more specific food recommendation experience compared to a simple Google search. Even if people don't want food recommendations, it's simply fun to see what you get given.
