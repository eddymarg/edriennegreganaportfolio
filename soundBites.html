01 — The Problem

Finding food is boring.

Every time I wanted to try somewhere new to eat, I'd end up staring blankly at Google Maps. The options felt overwhelming but generic. No personality, no vibe. Meanwhile, Spotify knows exactly what I'm in the mood for.

I started thinking: what if you could use your music taste to find food that actually fits how you're feeling? Your playlist already says a lot about you. Why not let it pick your next restaurant too?

02 — The Solution

SoundBites.

SoundBites connects to your Spotify account, reads your listening habits, and maps your music personality to a curated food recommendation. Not just a cuisine, but a full experience. Think: jazz lovers get a nice candlelit spot. Hyperpop fans get Korean fried chicken with neon lights.

The target audience is people who love Spotify Wrapped and personality quizzes. People who want a tailored experience, not another generic search.

03 — The Process

How it came together.

01

Research & Concept

I started by looking up what intricate genres existed and exploring the potential vibe for each one. From there, I tested queries with Google Maps to see how the results would come back.

02

Design in Figma

I built the full UI in Figma first, establishing the warm cream and coral palette, type hierarchy, and component system before writing a single line of React.

03

Build in React Vite

I built the web app using React Vite, integrating Spotify OAuth, parsing listening data, and building the recommendation logic that maps genres to food experiences.
