Saturn

While platforms like ChatGPT and Gemini are undeniably powerful, they felt a bit impersonal and lacked the sense of fun and creativity I was looking for. To change that and to challenge myself to learn React.js I created my own space-themed search platform inspired by Google Gemini. I customized the theming to reflect my personal style and design preferences, turning the project into both a learning experience and a creative outlet.
